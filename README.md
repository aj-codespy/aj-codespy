I was born in Bihar, grew up in Mumbai and started building in Pune in 2023, from zero: no web, no cloud, no AI. Two years later I'd won nine competitions, most of them hackathons and datathons, and was writing production agent code.

I like the whole problem, not just the ticket. I sit in on the user call, design the system, write the code and stay for what happens after launch. Outside of work, I run.

**Right now**

- **Working on** production multi-agent LLM systems on the AI team at Voiceqube
- **Building** HireLoop, a hiring OS where agents run structured voice interviews and score them against the role's rubric
- **Shipped** [getPlaced](https://getplaced.online), an AI resume tailor I built alone, used by 5,000+ people
- **Studying** B.E. AI & Data Science at MMCOE, Pune · GPA 9.3 · class of 2027
- **Open to** conversations about AI engineering roles, remote or relocation
